1. Who we are

ShweU (ရွှေဥ) is a Myanmar / English knowledge and community app for gold, gems and jewelry. It is operated by BLAKE ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your data in the app. Contact: blake@shweu.app.

2. Data we collect

Account

  • Email address and password (stored hashed by Supabase Auth), user ID and sign-in session tokens.

Profile (public)

  • Display name, bio, avatar, cover photo, role (user / creator / shop owner / admin), verified badge and account creation date.
  • For shop owners: shop name, city, phone, Viber, Telegram, Messenger link, map link, categories and shop item photos.
  • Profile links (up to 5): Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, Viber, website, phone number, map. Other users can see these, including phone numbers.

Content you post

  • Posts, articles (title and body in Myanmar and/or English, topic), Sparks videos and thumbnails, photos, reposts, comments and replies.
  • Post visibility (Public / Followers / Only me). Articles are always public and may go through admin review (review status and note).
  • Only photos and videos you pick in the system Photos picker are uploaded; we do not access your whole photo library.

Activity

  • Reactions, follows and favorites, saved posts, users you block, and reports you submit (category, details up to 500 characters, moderation record).

Creator / Shop applications

  • Display name, about text, sample and extra links, shop name, city, phone, Viber / Telegram, map link, and up to 5 files (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB each) you upload as proof. Admins review them and record the decision and a reason.

Moderation records

  • Role and badge change history, bans, legal holds and reports.

Error and crash reports

  • When something fails in the app (an error, a crash, a freeze, or heavy CPU / disk use reported by iOS), the app sends a short report: an error code and short message, the app and iOS version, and the time. If you are signed in, it is linked to your user ID. We use it only to fix problems; it is kept for 30 days and then deleted. It never contains your posts, messages, photos or password.

Stored only on your device

  • App and article language, the articles you have read, recent searches, and Gold Calculator settings. The Gold Calculator runs entirely on your device; nothing you enter is sent to us.

We do not collect: location (GPS), contacts, the advertising identifier (IDFA), analytics, payment data, or data for third-party crash reporting services or ads.

3. How we use it

  • To provide accounts, sign-in and the app's features (feed, profiles, follows, comments).
  • To show your content according to your visibility setting.
  • For safety and moderation (filters, reports, blocks, bans).
  • To review Creator / Shop applications.
  • To answer your requests.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not track you across apps or websites, so the app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — sign-in, database and file storage. Your data is stored and processed in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). supabase.com/privacy
  • gold-api.com and Binance public market data (data-api.binance.vision, api.binance.com) — only to fetch world gold prices. No personal data is sent; like any server, they can see your IP address.
  • Profile links you open (Facebook, Telegram and others) are governed by those sites' own policies.

5. Who can see it

  • Everyone: profiles, shop information, profile links, public posts, articles and comments, and follower counts. Avatars are public. Post photos and videos are kept in private storage and shown to permitted viewers through short-lived signed links.
  • Admins: reports, Creator / Shop applications, and content that needs review.
  • Application files are private: only you and admins can see them.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Delete in the app: Settings → Delete account, type "DELETE" and enter your password. Your profile, posts, articles, Sparks, comments, reactions, follows, saves, blocks, links, shop data, applications and all uploaded files (avatars, post photos and videos, shop item photos, application proof files) are deleted.
  • Reports you filed and role / badge change history may be kept anonymized (your ID removed). Others' reposts of your posts will show "not available".
  • If your account was banned when you delete it, we keep only a one-way hash of your email (not the email itself) and the ban terms, so a banned person cannot sign up again with the same email.
  • If an account is under a legal hold because of suspected illegal content, deletion is delayed while the hold lasts (normally up to 90 days), so evidence can be given to the authorities.
  • Copies may remain for a limited time in encrypted backups; they are not used.
  • If you cannot use the app, email blake@shweu.app from your account email to ask for deletion.

7. Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete posts, change visibility and block users at any time. For a copy of your data or a correction, email blake@shweu.app.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords and database Row Level Security. No system is 100% secure.

9. Children

ShweU is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that we have data from a child under 13, we delete it.

10. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the date above and tell you in the app about important changes. Questions: BLAKE, blake@shweu.app.
