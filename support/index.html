Support

We usually reply within 7 days.

Contact

Email blake@shweu.app, or in the app: Profile → Settings → Contact us.

Delete your account

Profile → Settings → Delete account → type DELETE and enter your password. Your account, posts, photos, videos and files are deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Report or block

Tap … on a post, comment or profile and choose Report or Block. Admins review reports every day. You no longer see content from people you block; unblock them in Settings → Blocked users.

For dangerous or illegal content, report it in the app and also email us.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password; we email you a link to set a new one.

Banned?

To appeal, email blake@shweu.app within 30 days from the email of your account. We reply within 7 days.

Become a Pro (Creator or Business)

Profile → Settings → Become a Creator or Business. Before approving, we check:

  • Creators: experience in your field (past work, a certificate or a social page), and that you post only content you made yourself.
  • Businesses: that the business is real (a photo of the shop or your work, plus a license or a business page link), and a phone number we can reach.
  • Everyone: the ShweU rules; no fake or misleading offers.

An admin decides within 7 days.

Pros, Discover and Favorites

In Posts: Pros shows posts from approved creators and businesses, Discover shows posts from everyone, and Favorites shows the accounts you added to Favorites. Pros are not endorsed by ShweU (see Terms §3).

Gold prices

Prices in the app are for reference only and are not financial advice.
